# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

## 1. What services does Rudrans Investment provide?

Rudrans Investment helps individuals and families achieve their financial goals through a range of investment solutions, including Mutual Funds, SIPs, ELSS, Tax-Saving Investments, Children's Education Planning, Retirement Planning, Wealth Creation, and Goal-Based Financial Planning. We recommend investment strategies based on your financial objectives, risk profile, and investment horizon.

---

## 2. Do you offer insurance or other financial products?

Yes. In addition to mutual fund investments, we can assist you with insurance solutions and connect you with trusted financial professionals for products such as health insurance, life insurance, loans, and other financial services whenever required.

---

## 3. How do you charge for your services?

Rudrans Investment is an AMFI Registered Mutual Fund Distributor. We receive distribution commissions directly from Asset Management Companies (AMCs) for regular mutual fund investments. There are no separate advisory charges for our distribution services. Our recommendations are always based on your financial needs and long-term investment goals.

---

## 4. Do you serve clients only in Surat?

A. No. While our office is based in Surat, Gujarat, we serve clients across PAN India through online consultations and digital investment platforms. We also work with NRI and international clients from various countries, providing reliable and personalized financial planning and investment services wherever they are.

---

## 5. Who can become a client?

We work with salaried professionals, business owners, NRIs, retirees, young investors, and families who are committed to building long-term wealth through disciplined investing. Whether you're just starting your investment journey or managing an established portfolio, we're here to help.

---

## 6. Is there a minimum investment amount?

No. You can begin investing with an SIP starting as low as ₹500 in many mutual fund schemes. We believe everyone deserves access to professional investment guidance, regardless of the investment amount.

---

## 7. Is my money safe when I invest through Rudrans Investment?

Absolutely. Your investments are made directly in your name with the respective Asset Management Companies (AMCs). Rudrans Investment never holds or controls your investment money. All transactions are securely processed through authorized banking channels and require your approval.

---

## 8. What if I decide to stop working with Rudrans Investment?

You are always in control of your investments. If you choose to discontinue our services, you may transfer your portfolio to another distributor, continue managing your investments independently, or redeem your investments according to applicable scheme terms.

---

## 9. What happens to my investments if Rudrans Investment closes its operations?

Your investments remain completely safe. They are held with the respective AMCs and regulated by SEBI and AMFI. Even if our business operations change in the future, your investments remain in your name and can be accessed or managed through the AMC or another authorized distributor.
